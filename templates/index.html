About EmotiSense AI

What is EmotiSense AI?

EmotiSense AI is an intelligent emotion detection system built with Python, Flask, and Machine Learning. It uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) to understand the emotional tone behind any piece of text.


Whether you're journaling, analyzing feedback, or just curious about your feelings — EmotiSense gives you real-time emotional insights powered by a Logistic Regression model trained on the HuggingFace Emotion Dataset.
